Privacy Terms

Delete Your Account

Applies to all Free the Borough games, including Blood in the Sand

To delete your account and everything connected to it, email us from the address you'd like us to reply to:

freetheborough.games@gmail.com

Please include the game you play and your in-game display name so we can find you. If you linked an Apple or Google sign-in, mention that too. We'll confirm once it's done — normally within a few days.

What gets deleted

Your player identity and all data held against it: currency balances and transaction history, unlocked and purchased items, ratings, match results, achievements, display name, and any linked Apple or Google sign-in (including your account at our sign-in provider). Deletion is permanent — purchases cannot be restored afterwards.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Short-lived server logs expire on their own, and we keep a bare record of the App Store / Google Play transaction IDs you redeemed — no longer linked to any player — so the same purchase can't be credited twice.

If you only want to remove a linked sign-in and keep playing anonymously, you can do that in-game under Settings without emailing us.
